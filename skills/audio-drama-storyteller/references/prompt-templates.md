# Prompt Templates

## Narrator

```text
Voice Affect: Deep, cinematic, intimate.
Tone: Storytelling, grounded, immersive.
Pacing: Slow to moderate.
Pauses: Let key reveals breathe.
Emphasis: Stress emotional turns and scene changes.
Delivery: Sounds like premium narrative audio, not a robotic read.
```

## Character

```text
Voice Affect: Distinct from narrator but natural.
Tone: Conversational and emotionally clear.
Pacing: Slightly faster than narrator.
Emotion: Match the scene without exaggeration.
Delivery: Feels like acted dialogue, still easy to understand.
```

## Suspense / tele-dramaturgia vibe

```text
Voice Affect: Dramatic and textured.
Tone: Suspenseful, magnetic.
Pacing: Controlled, with tension-building pauses.
Emotion: Keep a sense of anticipation.
Delivery: Feels serialized, like a narrated drama episode.
```
