# Instagram Strategist

Use this skill to design Instagram strategy with execution clarity.

## When to use

- Create a weekly or monthly Instagram plan.
- Define content pillars for brand or artist growth.
- Improve Reels/Stories/feed performance.
- Structure hook, narrative, and CTA patterns.
- Plan campaigns for launches, promos, and collaborations.

## Workflow

1. Clarify objective:
- Choose one primary objective: reach, followers, engagement, clicks, leads, or sales.

2. Define audience and positioning:
- Audience profile, pains/desires, tone, and angle of differentiation.

3. Build content system:
- 3 to 5 pillars.
- Format mix: Reels, Stories, Carousel, Static.
- Cadence by week.

4. Create post blueprint:
- Hook (first 1-2 seconds).
- Core message (value, proof, story).
- CTA (comment, DM, save, share, link).

5. Add growth loops:
- Collaboration plan.
- UGC or community prompts.
- Repurposing plan across formats.

6. Track and iterate:
- Track retention, saves, shares, profile visits, and follower conversion.
- Keep what performs; replace weak themes quickly.

## Output template

Return results in this structure:

- Objective and KPI.
- Audience and positioning.
- Content pillars.
- Weekly cadence (by day and format).
- 10 post ideas with hooks + CTA.
- Experiment list (A/B topics, hooks, durations).
- 2-week optimization plan.
